Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, whether New Forest Water Watch ("NFWW", "we", "us") uses them on our website, and how you can control them. Please read it alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your computer, phone or tablet when you visit it. They are used for many purposes, such as remembering your settings or helping a website work properly. Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies, which stay on your device for a set period or until you delete them

Cookies do not usually contain information that identifies you personally. However, where we hold personal information about you, it may be linked to information in a cookie. For more on how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies and similar storage set by our Website

Our Website does not set cookies. However, it does use your browser's local storage to remember choices you make, so you don't have to make them again. This includes your choices of filter and display settings. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove these at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Cookies set by services we use

Some services that we use may set cookies or similar technologies when you use the Website or its forms. These are listed below.

  • Tally form, used for collecting test submissions, see Tally's cookie policy
  • [Hosting provider], used for hosting the Website, see [Link]
  • JustGiving, used for accepting donations, see JustGiving's cookie policy

4. Future changes

If we decide to use cookies that are not essential to the Website, for example analytics cookies, we will ask for your consent before setting them. You will be able to accept or refuse them, and we will update this policy to say which cookies are used and why.

5. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies, and clear the information a website stores in your browser, through your browser settings. Each browser explains how:

  • Google Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari on Mac and Safari on iPhone and iPad

If you clear this information for our Website, the welcome window will appear again and the map will return to its default settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect how some parts of the Website work.
