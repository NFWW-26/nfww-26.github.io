New Forest Water Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how New Forest Water Watch ("NFWW", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our website, submit water test results, or contact us. It also explains your rights under UK data protection law.

1. Who we are

NFWW is a community-led volunteer initiative monitoring the health of rivers and streams across the New Forest. We are based in Lymington, UK, and we are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Contact us at newforestwaterwatch@gmail.com

2. Key terms

  • Personal data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable person.
  • Website means the New Forest Water Watch website, including its map and analysis pages.
  • Volunteer means anyone who takes part in water sampling for NFWW, whether as an individual or within a local group.
  • Service providers means the organisations listed in section 6 that process personal data on our behalf.

3. Information we collect

From volunteers and people who submit results (through our sampling form, which is run using Tally):

  • For quality assurance and follow up
    • name of the tester or testers, where given
    • email address
  • For scientific analysis and reporting
    • date, time and location of the sample, including site code and coordinates
    • test results and observations
    • photos, where uploaded

From people who contact us by email or through the website: your name, email address and the content of your message.

Technical information when you visit the Website: our hosting provider, and the services listed in section 6, may automatically record your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of your visit. We do not use analytics or advertising tracking. [Confirm with the hosting provider and check the Tally form.]

We do not collect payment details. Donations are made through JustGiving, which has its own privacy policy.

4. How we use your information and our legal basis

  • To run water sampling and publish the results. Our legal basis is legitimate interests: monitoring freshwater health in the public interest. Where we publish names of testers, our basis is your consent (see section 5).
  • To contact you about sampling, and to manage volunteer records. Legitimate interests.
  • To reply to your messages. Legitimate interests, or steps you asked us to take before entering into an arrangement with you.
  • To keep the Website secure and working. Legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal obligations, for example where a law requires us to disclose information.

5. Publication of results

Water quality results are published openly on our map and analysis pages. Published information includes the site name, catchment, location, date, time, results and observations.

Email addresses of volunteers are never published. When photos are uploaded, volunteers should obtain the permission of anyone who is identifiable in a photograph before uploading, and are asked to agree to any uploaded photos being later made available for public view. By uploading a photo using our data collection form, the volunteer is implicitly agreeing for the photo to be later made available for public view. If you have uploaded a photo and subsequently decide that you don’t agree to this, please contact us and we will delete it.

Where a sample is not matched to a monitoring site, its exact position is shown as it was recorded. If you take samples from private land and would prefer the location not to be published, please contact us and we will review it.

Published results are shared under the licence set out in our Terms of Use. Our partners (see section 6) may also use the results.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the following:

  • Tally: runs our sampling form and stores the submissions.
  • Google (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Apps Script): stores the submissions and produces the website pages.
  • [Name of website hosting provider]: hosts the Website and keeps server logs.
  • Map and chart services used by the Website: unpkg.com, cdnjs.cloudflare.com (code libraries), and Esri, OpenStreetMap & OpenTopoMap (map tiles). When a page loads, these services receive your IP address.
  • FLOW CIC, WaterCAN and Water Rangers UK: our partners in freshwater monitoring. Information is shared with them under agreements that require them to protect it.
  • Public authorities, where required by law.

7. International transfers

Some of our service providers, including Google and Tally, may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, they rely on safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

8. How long we keep information

  • Contact messages: 12 months, unless we need them for longer to deal with an ongoing matter.
  • Submission records containing names and email addresses: for as long as the volunteer takes part, or until the volunteer requests records to be removed, at which point they are anonymised by removing names and email addresses.
  • Anonymised results (with names and email addresses removed): kept indefinitely as a long-term record of water quality.
  • Server logs: kept by the hosting provider in line with its own policy.

9. Young people

Some volunteers may be under 18, for example through youth groups or schools. If a person under 18 takes part, we ask that a parent, guardian or group leader submits the results on their behalf, and we do not publish the young person's name.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected information from a child without consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Cookies

Our Website does not set cookies, but stores your map choices in your browser. Please see our Cookies Policy for details, including any third-party services that may set cookies.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • ask us to delete your information, where we no longer need it
  • ask us to restrict or object to how we use it
  • withdraw your consent, where we rely on consent
  • ask for a copy of your information in a portable format, where it applies

To use any of these rights, contact us at newforestwaterwatch@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. Please note that anonymised results already published cannot be linked back to you, so we may be unable to remove them.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

13. Links to other websites

The Website links to other websites, including our blog, our resources page and JustGiving. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. Please read their privacy policies before providing any information to them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make a significant change, we will tell you by email where we can, or by a notice on the Website. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when the policy was last revised.

15. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
